PicForge applies version-specific pnpm patches to @jsquash/avif 2.1.1 and
@jsquash/oxipng 2.3.0 JavaScript initialization wrappers to retain single-thread
encoding under cross-origin isolation. Patch sources are in the repository's
patches/ directory. The WASM binaries and upstream license notices are unchanged.

PicForge also patches wasm-vips 0.0.18 browser ES-module glue to cap the Emscripten
pthread pool and VIPS_MAX_THREADS together at six. Pool exhaustion returns EAGAIN
instead of allocating another Worker. The original vips.wasm bytes and upstream
notices are preserved. See patches/wasm-vips@0.0.18.patch and docs/phase4-validation.md.
